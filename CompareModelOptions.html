Linear Model with correlated factors

So this is an option Daniel and I talked through, trying a regular linear model with factors from the pre-selected CpGs that have high linear correlation with otolith age. Overall, I ended up with pretty similar predictions and MAE to elastic net, but it looks like it doesn’t have the same issue with overestimating young individuals and underestimating older individuals.
